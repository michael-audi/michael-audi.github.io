About Me

I’m usually working on something.

I started programming in elementary school, making games and simulations in Scratch. What began as something I did for fun gradually became one of the main ways I explore the things I'm interested in.

Over the years, I've moved from Scratch into Python, Java, JavaScript, and other tools, but the way I approach projects hasn't changed much. I find something interesting, try to understand how it works, and usually end up building my own version of it.

That's led me to projects ranging from the physics simulation at the top of this page to a Block Blast solver, a chess engine, automation tools, and mathematical animations.
