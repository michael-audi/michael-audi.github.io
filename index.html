About Me

I’m drawn to the point where curiosity turns into something I can build.

A lot of my projects begin with a question I don’t know how to answer. I’ll get curious about a system, an algorithm, or a mathematical idea, and instead of leaving it there, I usually try to recreate it myself.

That started with Scratch projects and physics simulations, and over time grew into Java, Python, JavaScript, chess engines, puzzle solvers, automation tools, and mathematical visualizations.

What I enjoy most is the combination of mathematics and computer science: taking something abstract and turning it into something interactive, visual, or useful.
